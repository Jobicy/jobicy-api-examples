# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free contributor community. Participate with empathy, accept constructive feedback, credit the work of others, and focus disagreements on ideas rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of another person's private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report harmful conduct privately through [Jobicy's official contact channels](https://jobicy.com/contact-us). Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy follows the core principles of the Contributor Covenant.
